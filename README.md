**Elevator Pitch (100 words max):**

Flipside is a 2D minimalistic pixel art platformer that challenges players to navigate dynamically shifting environments. With a single input, the player toggles between two distinct map states, each with unique platforms, traps, and collectibles. This switch mechanic serves as a puzzle solving and sometimes a timing based challenge, requiring strategic thinking and quick reflexes. The game emphasizes pattern recognition and adaptability to successfully clear the levels.

---

**Game Synopsis, Objective, and Mechanics:**

Flipside features levels constructed from two overlapping layers, each with a difference in terrain. The player's main objective is to traverse through the level and proceed to the exit by switching layers to reveal safe paths and avoid hazards. Each time a layer switch occurs the stage layout changes in realm time at the press of a button, turning static layouts in a dyanmic puzzle.

Level design emphasizes readability and problem solving. As players progress, new mechanics such as moving hazards, timing jumps, and hidden collectibles and dangers accessible only after a switch has occurred. The minimalistic art style supports clear visibility of platform states, reinforcing the game’s focus on timing and puzzle solving.
