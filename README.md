**Elevator Pitch (100 words max):**

Flipside is a 2D minimalistic pixel art platformer that challenges players to navigate dynamically shifting environments. With a single input, the player toggles between two distinct map states, each with unique platforms, traps, and collectibles. This switch mechanic serves as a puzzle solving and sometimes a timing based challenge, requiring strategic thinking and quick reflexes. The game emphasizes pattern recognition and adaptability to successfully clear the levels.

---

**Game Synopsis, Objective, and Mechanics:**

Flipside presents a layered world composed of two parallel sides. The core objective is to traverse each level by leveraging the side switching mechanic to manipulate the terrain. Each side variation offers different paths, obstacles, and collectibles, forcing players to plan movements and anticipate environmental shifts. The minimalistic visual style ensures clarity while emphasizing gameplay over complexity. Progression introduces new hazards and timing puzzles that deepen the player’s mastery of switching rhythm and spatial awareness.
