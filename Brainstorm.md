For the game that I want to build, since I am going with a 2D platformer I was thinking of making it so the players progresses through multiple levels. The following level could just be the user heading to the right or I can also create split paths that offer exploration for the players. This way I can have a locked door and the players will have to figure out how to get it open. Instead of a locked door I could also do something where the problem would need a new mechanic to solve, like wall clinging or a double jump. I could also add a glide and/or dash mechanic to the game but I'm not sure how to implement that yet so we'll have to see when we get there. If I do have enough time and I am ahead on my project, I would also like to add combat to the game. Personally I like parry mechanics in metroidvanias so that could be something interesting to implement. The attacks can also be for more purposes than one and allow the player to pogo on the enemy. Pogoing is you jump and hit the enemy below you while above them to bounce yourself without expending another jump charge. This would allow more movement options and the players would also have to think before just killing off enemy entities. This means that I would have to implement a way to have the enemy entities reset upon request like the player leaving the area and coming back or a button to reset a level. Experimenting with secret areas and a point system would be interesting too so the player has something to collect instead of just thinking about going to the next level which can add a degree of challenge.
