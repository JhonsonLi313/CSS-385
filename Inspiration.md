Deciding on a singular game genre was quite difficult but I think I will be doing a 2D platformer in Unity or Godot. 2D platformers is definitely one of the game types that I enjoy, metroidvanias would fall into this category as well like Hollowknight. Given our 10 week limit I also feel like this would be ideal genre for me to go for. This genre allows me to also explore different game mechanics like momentum, gravity, and even exploration. A lot of 2D platformers involve progressing through the level with just jumps until you unlock new abilities like wall climb, double jump, or sprinting if not already given to the player. These mechanics adds on to the existing mechanics to create a more challenging experience and allows players the chance to problem solve their way through a stage. This also means that as a developer I would be able to play around with these mechanics and see how to make the game form cohesively.
